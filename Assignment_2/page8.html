Mango Shake

Ingredients:

  • 1 ripe mango (peeled and chopped)
  • 1/2 cup milk (or any plant-based milk like coconut, almond, etc.)
  • 1/2 cup vanilla or plain yogurt (optional, for creaminess)
  • 1-2 tsp honey or sweetener (optional)
  • 1/4 tsp cardamom powder (optional, for a hint of spice)
  • Ice cubes (optional, for a chilled shake)

Instructions:

  1. Prepare the Mango: Peel and chop the mango into pieces.
  2. Blend: Add the mango, milk, yogurt (if using), and sweetener to a blender. Add ice cubes if you want a cooler shake.
  3. Blend Until Smooth: Blend everything until smooth and creamy.
  4. Taste and Adjust: Taste the shake and add more sweetener or cardamom if desired.
  5. Serve: Pour into a glass, and enjoy the tropical goodness!
