Paneer Tikka

Ingredients:

  • 250g paneer (cubed)
  • 2 tbsp yogurt
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp red chili powder, garam masala, turmeric
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 tbsp cornflour (optional)

Instructions:

  1. Mix yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, lemon juice, oil, spices, and salt.
  2. Marinate paneer cubes for at least 30 minutes.
  3. Grill in the oven at 200°C for 15-20 minutes, or pan-fry until golden.
  4. Serve with lemon wedges and onion rings.
